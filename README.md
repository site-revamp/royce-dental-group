# Royce Dental Group — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Royce Dental Group is a Singapore dental group established in 2004, providing general dentistry and specialist treatments across Singapore. Its website helps patients explore treatments, find dentists and clinics, and arrange appointments.

The redesigned homepage prioritises patient navigation, explains appointment channels before booking and provides consistent access to specialist information.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Appointment Routes

The homepage separates general dental booking from specialist and other appointment enquiries, with confirmation expectations beside each route.

Online booking restrictions are explained upfront: general consultations, cleaning and simple fillings can be booked online, while braces, Invisalign, specialist, urgent and same-day appointments require direct contact. Previously, the generic booking action did not communicate these limitations.

### 2. Patient-Focused Navigation

Primary navigation now prioritises Treatments, Dentists, Clinics, Patient Information and Appointments. Professional, corporate and recruitment links move to secondary navigation.

This addresses the previous nine-category header, which mixed different audiences and wrapped its final category onto another line at the inspected desktop width.

### 3. Consistent Specialist Links

Each specialist route uses a clear, verified destination and descriptive call to action. This fixes inconsistent gum-specialist navigation, including a link to a missing homepage anchor, helping patients reach the intended specialist information.

## Design Improvements

- **Layout and hierarchy:** Patient tasks take priority in the main navigation.
- **Content clarity:** Appointment restrictions and confirmation expectations appear beside booking choices.
- **Consistency:** Specialist links use clear destinations and descriptive labels.
- **User experience:** Patients can identify the appropriate appointment route with less uncertainty.
