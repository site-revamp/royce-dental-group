A few things to know

Can I book a specialist appointment online?

The online booking page is for general consultations, cleaning and simple fillings with general dentists. For braces, Invisalign or specialist care, contact the clinic directly. The concierge can help with specialist enquiries.

Is an online appointment confirmed immediately?

No. Royce states that the clinic will contact you within 24 hours to confirm the appointment. Please wait for confirmation of your date and time.

How do I find a gum specialist?

Visit the Periodontists / Gum Specialists section of the doctors page, or ask the concierge about an appointment enquiry.
